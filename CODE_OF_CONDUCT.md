# Code of Conduct

Series of Tubes is a cartoon game about how the internet works, built by people who
love networks and people who are just curious about them. Everyone is welcome here:
seasoned network engineers, students, translators, players who have never heard of BGP,
and people who just want to suggest a city with a funny data center name.

The short version: **be excellent to each other.**

## Where this applies

Everywhere people meet around this project: GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions, the Series of Tubes Signal group, and anywhere else you represent the
project, online or at a conference bar after NANOG.

## What we want

- **Welcome people.** Newcomers are how every community stays alive. Nobody was born
  knowing what a route reflector is. Explain things kindly, and assume the question is
  sincere.
- **Be patient across languages.** Many of us write in our second or third language.
  Judge the idea, not the grammar.
- **Disagree about ideas, not people.** Argue about peering policy, balance and puns as
  hard as you like. Criticise the change, never the person who made it.
- **Assume good intent.** Text loses tone. If something reads badly, ask before you
  react.
- **Give credit.** Thank people for reports, fixes and ideas, and say where an idea came
  from.
- **Own your mistakes.** We all fat-finger a config now and then. Apologise, fix it,
  move on.
- **Keep the jokes kind.** This game runs on puns. The target of a joke is a network,
  a cable or a router, never a person or a group of people.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's gender, gender identity or
  expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body, age, race, ethnicity,
  nationality, religion, experience level or anything else about who they are.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, or encouraging anyone else to do these things.
- Sharing someone's private information (an address, a phone number, a real name they
  have not shared) without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or flooding issues and chats.
- Continuing to contact someone after they have asked you to stop.

## If something happens

If someone makes you or others feel unwelcome or unsafe, tell the maintainer, Frederic,
in a private message in the Signal group. You do not need to be the person it happened
to, and you do not need proof or perfect wording. Reports are kept confidential.

We will listen, look at what happened, and do what fits: a friendly word, a request to
edit or delete something, a warning, or removing someone from the project's spaces for a
while or for good. Whoever reports in good faith will never be punished for it.

## Making it better

This document is for everyone here. If something is missing or unclear, open an issue
or a pull request, like for any other part of the project.
